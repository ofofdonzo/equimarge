Equimarge is a compliance consulting firm in France (GDPR / outsourced Data Protection Officer, anti-money-laundering,
AI and anti-corruption compliance) for small businesses and insurance intermediaries. This app is the client portal:
the consultant performs the compliance mission, the client follows it and provides what is needed.

What to try:
1. Home tab ("Accueil"): mission progress, items to provide, upcoming deadlines.
2. Requests tab ("Demandes"): answer a request and attach a document (file picker or camera).
3. Documents tab: open a deliverable (opens in an in-app Safari view).
4. Messages tab: write to the consultant; new messages arrive in real time.
5. Account (round button, top right): enable notifications, privacy policy, sign out,
   and "Supprimer mon compte" (account deletion, guideline 5.1.1(v)) — type SUPPRIMER to confirm.

Native features: push notifications for new requests, deliverables and messages (permission is requested only when
the user taps "Activer les notifications"; the app works fully without it), camera capture of documents, in-app Safari
view for documents, tab bar navigation, offline notice. All screens are bundled in the app; no website is loaded in the
app.

The app is free and sells no digital content. The consulting service itself is quoted and invoiced outside the app and
performed by the consultant outside the app (guideline 3.1.3(e)). Sign in uses our own account system only
(email and password), so Sign in with Apple is not required (guideline 4.8).
